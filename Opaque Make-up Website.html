About Opaque

A Legacy of Beauty & Innovation

Our Philosophy

At Opaque, we believe makeup is more than beauty—it's an art form, a statement, and a celebration of individuality. Founded with a vision to blend luxury with creativity, we craft products that empower you to express your unique style.

Every product in our collection is thoughtfully designed, from the elegant rose gold finishes to the richly pigmented formulas. We source the finest ingredients and work with master artisans to ensure each piece meets our exacting standards.

Our commitment extends beyond beauty. We're dedicated to sustainable practices, cruelty-free testing, and creating products that honor both our customers and our planet.
